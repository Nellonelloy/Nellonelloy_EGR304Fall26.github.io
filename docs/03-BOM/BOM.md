## Overview

The Bill of Materials (BOM) provides a detailed list of the components required to construct my pressure-sensing subsystem for the EGR 304 environmental monitoring project. The design incorporates a PIC18F57Q43 microcontroller, XGZP pressure sensor, MCP6004 operational amplifier, 5V voltage regulator, and supporting electrical components. The BOM identifies component specifications, quantities, manufacturers, suppliers, and costs to support accurate purchasing, PCB assembly, and future maintenance or modifications.
