    Table 1: component selection

    Op-Amp

    Solution Pros Cons

    Option 1.
    TLV2464CN Operational Amplifier
    $4.50/each
    link to product    		 * Very low input offset
    * Low current input bias
    * Meets surface mount constraint of project    		 * Much more expensive than other options
    * Low operating temperature
    * Voltage supply minimum fairly high

    * Option 2.
    * TL974IN Operational Amplifier
    * $1.66/each
    * Link to product    		 * Very good voltage supply max
    * Stable over operating temperature
    * Good voltage input offset    		 * Very bad current input bias
    * Bad Slew rate (not very important in my utilization)

    Option 3.
    MCP6004-I/P Operational Amplifier
    $0.60/each
    link to product    		 * Very low cheap
    * very good current input bias
    * Good voltage supply minimum    		 * Bad voltage input offset
    * Bad current output (not very important for my application)
    Choice: Option 3: MCP6004-I/P Operational Amplifier

    Rationale: The Op-Amp I chose is cheap, and it has a low voltage minimum supply. The biggest weakness of this one is the voltage input offset, but since we are going for an update every 2 seconds this is a sacrifice that I feel will have negiligble inpact.

    Voltage Regulator

    Solution Pros Cons

    Option 1.
    L7805ABV-DG Voltage Regulator
    $1.53/each
    link to product    		 * Through Hole like other componenets
    * High current output
    * Meets surface mount constraint of project    		 * Much higher voltage dropout
    * More expensive
    * Less protection features

    * Option 2.
    * AP7583Q-50D4-13 Voltage Regulator
    * $0.95/each
    * Link to product    		 *Low cost
    * Low voltage dropout
    * Fixed 5V output    		 * Surface mount and not through hole
    *Four prong instead of three (very unimportant other than increasing mounting time)

    Option 3.
    AP7583AQ-50D4-13 Voltage Regulator
    $1.01/each
    link to product    		 * Cheap
    * 5V fixed ouput
    * Multiple control features    		 * Not through hole
    * Four prong instead of three (very unimportant other than increasing mounting time)
    Choice: Option 1: L7805ABV-DG Voltage Regulator

    Rationale: The voltage regulator I chose is a bit more expensive, but it's footprint is more compatible with my PCB than the others, and the advantages the other voltage regulators have over this one are of negligible difference for the purpose of this project.

    Pressure Sensor

    Solution Pros Cons

    Option 1.
    CFSensor XGZP6878A15115KPA Pressure Sensor
    $7.20/each
    link to product    		 * Cheap
    * Pressure range covers needed levels
    		 * 2.5% margin of error worse than original requirements
    * Already amplified making op amp unecessary

    * Option 2.
    Honeywell Sensing and Productivity Solutions 13U1000PA0K
    Pressure Sensor
    * $363.96/each
    * Link to product    		 *Accuracy within project bounds
    * Compatible with 5V power rail
    * Allows use of chosen op amp    		 * Extremely expensive
    *Out of stock at the supplier

    Option 3.
    WEC 13U0500PA0K pressure sensor
    $195.18/each
    link to product    		 * Allows use of op-amp
    * 0.25% margin of error
    * Compatible with 5v power rail    		 * Extremely expensive
    * Large metal package
    Choice: Option 1: CFSensor XGZP6878A15115KPA Pressure Sensor

    Rationale: This choice does not require an op-amp, and it is the only affordable option that was available that was also in stock.

    Power-Supply

    Solution Pros Cons

    Option 1.
    YINGJIAO YS6V-0900500-D1M power supply
    $7.64/each
    link to product    		 * Cheap
    * Large onput voltage range
    *Compact    		 * Low maximum output current
    * Minimum order quanitity of 2

    * Option 2.
    * Pololu 1470 power supply
    * $11.41/each
    * Link to product    		 * High current ouput
    * Regulated 9V output
    * Wide input voltage range    		 * Moderately expensive
    * Shorter power cord

    Option 3.
    Jameco ReliaPro GPU410900500WD00 Power Supply
    $13.24/each
    link to product    		 * Linear design
    * Regulated 9V output
    *Multiple safety approvals    		 * Expensive
    * Only accepts 120V AC input
    Choice: Option 2: Pololu 1470 Power Supply

    Rationale: The power supply I chose has a regulated 9V output, high current capacity, and compatibility with my voltage regulator. The 1A output will be useful if there are any future modifications to the design.
